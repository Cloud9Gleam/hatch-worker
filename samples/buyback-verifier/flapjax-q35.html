Method & limits

  • Discovery transport: seeds — SEED-BASED — every listed buy was verified from raw chain receipts, but the window was NOT independently enumerated (no logs-capable endpoint); completeness rests on the seed list supplied by the caller.
  • Every figure is re-derived from raw chain data (eth_getTransactionByHash, eth_getTransactionReceipt, eth_getBlockByNumber) via public BSC RPC. Token amounts are the exact ERC-20 Transfer log values (wei-exact), never explorer/index figures.
  • Log enumeration is chunked: public BSC endpoints reject eth_getLogs ranges above roughly 1,000 blocks (error -32005), so scans walk the window in adaptive chunks and record any range that could not be fetched.
  • Scope is the single pool above: buys routed through other pools or aggregators, and direct non-swap transfers into the pool, are outside this report. Gas fees paid by the treasury for these buys: 0.0000573319 BNB.
